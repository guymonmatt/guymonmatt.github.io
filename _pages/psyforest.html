Psychedelic Forest

This page reads your camera feed on your device — nothing is ever uploaded or stored — and grows a 3D forest of glowing, color-shifting flowers around you. The palette is seeded from your face and shifts with your expression, so it looks a little different for everyone.

Requires camera access and WebGL. Works best in Chrome or Safari on a phone.
