Driftwheel

A slow-moving ambient synth pad. Spin the wheels to change the root note, the chord type, and the synth tone — flip on the arp to have it pick out the notes of whatever chord you're holding, or add wind and twinkling chimes for texture underneath.

Turn your volume up. Works best with headphones.
